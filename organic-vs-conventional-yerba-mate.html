Published February 2026 • 7 min read

Limitations of Current Research

Most studies are small-scale or limited to lab analyses. Human clinical data directly comparing health outcomes for organic vs conventional yerba mate are limited. Consumer choices may depend more on safety concerns than measurable nutrient differences.
