Benefits of Yerba Mate Backed by Research

Published February 2026 • 8 min read

Yerba mate is more than a traditional South American drink. Modern research reveals multiple potential health benefits, including antioxidant support, cognitive improvement, cardiovascular health, and metabolic effects.

Human Trials & Research

  • Cardiovascular benefits: yerba mate reduces LDL cholesterol in human trials
  • Antioxidant effects in plasma after daily consumption
  • Cognitive enhancement and polyphenols study
  • Metabolic effects: improved glucose regulation
  • Weight management and energy expenditure trial

Comparison vs Green Tea

Property Yerba Mate Green Tea
Antioxidants (ORAC) High Moderate-High
Caffeine Moderate (70 mg per cup) Moderate (30–50 mg per cup)
Polyphenols High High
Fat Oxidation Boost Yes (some trials) Yes (limited trials)
Traditional Use South America, daily social drink Asia, meditation and health rituals

Limitations of Current Research

While human trials suggest promising benefits, sample sizes are often small, durations are short, and many studies are sponsored by producers. More independent, long-term studies are needed.
