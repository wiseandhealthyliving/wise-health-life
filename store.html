Apothecary Safety & Storage

Herbs are powerful — treat them with the same respect you would any medicine.

Storage Rules

  • Cool & Dark: Keep in a cupboard, away from heat and direct sunlight.
  • Airtight: Use glass jars with tight-fitting lids to prevent oxidation.
  • Labeled: Always note the herb name, Latin name, and purchase date.
  • Shelf Life: Most dried herbs are best within 1–2 years. Trust your nose.

Safety First

  • Always check for drug interactions if you take prescription medication.
  • Verify pregnancy safety for each herb before use.
  • Discard any herb that shows moisture, mold, or off-smell.
  • Less is more — start with small doses and observe your body.

Consult a Professional

  • Nothing on this site replaces professional medical advice.
  • If you have chronic illness, consult a herbalist or integrative doctor.
  • Children and the elderly may need different dosing guidance.
  • Some herbs are contraindicated with surgery — disclose all supplements.

⚠️ Before You Visit Amazon

Herbs are powerful and natural — but they're not without risks. Please consult a healthcare provider before starting any new herbal protocol, especially if pregnant or on medication.
