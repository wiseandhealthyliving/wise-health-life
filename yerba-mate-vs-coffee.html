Yerba Mate vs Coffee: Which Is Healthier?

Published February 2026 • 8 min read

Many people love coffee for its energy kick, but yerba mate is gaining popularity as a natural, nutrient-rich alternative. Let’s explore the differences and which may be healthier for your body.

Nutritional & Caffeine Comparison

Feature Yerba Mate Coffee
Caffeine per cup 30–50 mg 95–120 mg
Antioxidants High (polyphenols, saponins) High (chlorogenic acids)
Effect on energy Smooth, longer-lasting Quick spike, possible jitters
Micronutrients Magnesium, Potassium, Vitamins B & C Minimal

Human Trials & Research

  • Yerba mate: antioxidant & metabolic effects
  • Yerba mate: cardiovascular support
  • Coffee: cognitive performance & antioxidants
  • Coffee: cardiovascular effects
  • Yerba mate: cognitive function & polyphenols

Limitations of Current Research

Most studies are short-term or have small sample sizes. Individual tolerance to caffeine varies. Direct long-term comparisons between yerba mate and coffee are limited.
