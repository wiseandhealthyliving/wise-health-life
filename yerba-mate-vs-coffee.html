Yerba Mate vs Coffee: Which Is Healthier?

Published February 2026 • 7 min read

Yerba mate and coffee are two of the world’s most popular caffeinated beverages. Both have antioxidants and health-promoting compounds, but their effects differ. Here’s a detailed science-backed comparison.

Caffeine Content & Energy

  • Coffee: ~95 mg per 8 oz cup
  • Yerba Mate: ~65 mg per 8 oz cup

Yerba mate contains theobromine, which provides a smoother energy boost than coffee’s quick spike and crash.

Antioxidants & Polyphenols

  • Yerba mate: Chlorogenic acids, saponins, xanthines [PubMed]
  • Coffee: Chlorogenic acids, caffeic acid, diterpenes [PubMed]

Human Trials & Observed Benefits

  1. Yerba mate may improve lipid profiles and reduce LDL cholesterol [PubMed]
  2. Moderate yerba mate consumption associated with improved glycemic control in type 2 diabetes [PubMed]
  3. Yerba mate improves antioxidant status in overweight individuals [PubMed]
  4. Coffee consumption linked to reduced risk of neurodegenerative diseases [PubMed]
  5. Coffee also improves short-term cognitive function and alertness [PubMed]

Limitations of Current Research

Most human trials are small, short-term, or focused on specific populations. Results may not apply to everyone, and long-term safety and efficacy require more robust studies.
