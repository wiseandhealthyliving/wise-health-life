Yerba Mate vs Coffee: Which Is Healthier?

Published February 2026 • 8 min read

Coffee is a staple worldwide, but yerba mate is gaining popularity for its unique nutrient profile. Here’s how the two compare for health, energy, and wellness.

Human Trials & Research

  • Yerba Mate lowers LDL cholesterol and improves cardiovascular markers
  • Plasma antioxidant levels increased with daily yerba mate intake
  • Cognitive performance and polyphenol study with yerba mate
  • Coffee consumption improves alertness and memory in short-term trials
  • Coffee intake associated with lower risk of some chronic diseases

Comparison Table: Yerba Mate vs Coffee

Property Yerba Mate Coffee
Caffeine per 8 oz cup ~70 mg ~95 mg
Antioxidants (ORAC) High High
Polyphenols High High
Cardiovascular support May lower LDL, improve circulation May reduce risk of type 2 diabetes and stroke
Cognitive effects Smoother focus, less jittery Rapid alertness, potential jitters

Limitations of Current Research

Most yerba mate studies are small, short-term, or sponsored by producers. Coffee research is larger but can be influenced by preparation methods. Individual responses vary. More independent, long-term human trials are needed.
