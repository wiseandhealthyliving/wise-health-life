How to Brew Yerba Mate Properly: Step-by-Step Guide

Published February 2026 • 6 min read

Brewing yerba mate correctly ensures maximum flavor, antioxidants, and a pleasant, smooth experience. Follow these steps for traditional and modern preparation.

Traditional Method (Gourd & Bombilla)

  1. Fill a gourd about 2/3 with yerba mate.
  2. Cover the opening with your hand, tilt, and shake gently to position fine particles on top.
  3. Insert a clean bombilla (metal straw with filter) at an angle.
  4. Pour hot water (70–80°C / 160–175°F) over the leaves.
  5. Sip slowly; refill with water multiple times as needed.

Modern Method (French Press or Teapot)

  1. Add 1–2 tablespoons of yerba mate per cup into a French press or teapot.
  2. Pour hot water (not boiling) over leaves.
  3. Steep for 3–5 minutes depending on desired strength.
  4. Press or strain, then serve.

Cold Brewing Yerba Mate

Place loose yerba mate in cold water and refrigerate 4–6 hours. This produces a milder, less bitter tea while preserving antioxidants.

Tips & Dos and Don’ts

  • Do use filtered water for better flavor.
  • Don’t use boiling water to avoid bitterness.
  • Do rinse the gourd before first use (traditional method) to remove dust.
  • Do experiment with ratios and steeping times to suit taste.
