How to Brew Yerba Mate Properly

Published February 2026 • 7 min read

Brewing yerba mate correctly ensures you get the maximum antioxidants, vitamins, and smooth caffeine release without bitterness. Follow this research-backed guide for the perfect cup every time.

Step-by-Step Brewing Guide

  1. Choose your yerba mate: Organic is preferable to reduce pesticide exposure.
  2. Prepare your gourd or cup: Fill about 1/2 to 2/3 with yerba mate leaves.
  3. Tilt the gourd: Angle it to create a slope, keeping leaves on one side.
  4. Add water: Pour hot water (70–80°C / 160–175°F) over the leaves. Avoid boiling water.
  5. Insert your bombilla: Drink slowly, refilling with hot water 2–3 times.
  6. Optional flavors: Add citrus, mint, or honey to taste without overwhelming natural compounds.

Brewing Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using boiling water (over 85°C) makes it bitter.
  • Overfilling the gourd reduces proper infusion.
  • Using poor-quality yerba mate can diminish health benefits.
